# Privacy Policy — Gulf AI Deals
Last updated: 13 October 2026

Gulf AI Deals (gulfaideals.com) is a free, read-only information hub. We do not require accounts and do not collect names, emails or payment data.

**MCP server, API and plugins** (/mcp, /openapi.json): search queries are processed only to return results. We do not store query content linked to any person and do not sell or share data. Our host (Cloudflare) may process standard request logs (IP address, timestamp, user agent) for security and reliability under its own privacy policy.

**Cookies:** none for tracking or advertising.

**Third parties:** results link to public external sources; their policies apply when you visit them.

**Children:** not directed at children under 13.

**Changes** will be posted on this page. Contact: via the contact link on gulfaideals.com.
